Copy check

Found one of our sheets somewhere else? Give a screenshot or photo of one page. This page reads the hidden dot pattern and shows which download it came from. Nothing is uploaded; it is read on this phone.

  • Best: a straight screenshot of a page. A cropped part of a page also works, it just takes longer.
  • Not yet: a tilted photo of a printed page.
  • Download numbers start being added to sheets once marked downloads go live; older PDFs have no number.
